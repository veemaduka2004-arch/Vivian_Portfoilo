More About Me

Curiosity Led Me Here. Creativity Keeps Me Building.

My name is Vivian Maduka, and I am an aspiring Front-End Developer with a passion for technology, creativity and building meaningful digital experiences.

My web development journey began with a desire to understand how websites work and how ideas can be transformed into interactive digital products.

Over six months of structured learning, I developed a foundation in HTML, CSS, SCSS and JavaScript, exploring everything from document structure and responsive layouts to programming fundamentals, modern JavaScript syntax and asynchronous operations.

I have also applied my knowledge by developing my personal portfolio website, giving me practical experience in turning concepts into working web pages.

I enjoy learning, experimenting with new ideas, solving problems and continuously improving my development skills.

My goal is to grow into a highly capable Front-End Developer who creates accessible, functional and visually engaging web experiences.
